# AgriN & Regenerative Agricultural Intelligence
> **Multilingual AI-Powered Agricultural Intelligence Platform for the BRICS Innovation Challenge**

AgriN connects grassroots farmer feedback with agricultural, environmental, soil, water, infrastructure, and demographic data. It identifies regional need hotspots and provides evidence-based information for government officials reviewing development allocations and climate adaptation plans.

---

## 🌾 Key Highlights

1. **Multilingual Voice & Text Intake**:
   - Smallholder farmers can report problems via spoken voice or text in their mother tongue (Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Russian, Portuguese, Chinese, etc.).
   - Eliminates digital literacy barriers through speech recognition and domain-specific agricultural Natural Language Understanding (NLU).

2. **AI Agricultural Taxonomy & Data Fusion**:
   - Automatically classifies complaints into categories (Water & Irrigation, Soil Health, Infrastructure, Market Access, Storage, Crop Disease).
   - Cross-correlates human grievances with satellite soil moisture (Sentinel-2 / SMAP), rainfall anomalies, and aquifer drawdown metrics.

3. **Geospatial Hotspot Map**:
   - Interactive GIS-style dashboard highlighting regional crisis pockets across Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, and Madhya Pradesh.

4. **Regenerative Agriculture Intelligence**:
   - Six systemic regenerative pillars: Soil Health, Water Efficiency, Crop Diversity, Crop Rotation, Agroforestry, and Soil Conservation.
   - Tailored regional profiles (e.g., Rajasthan Thar basin) providing scientifically grounded areas for consideration.

5. **Government Decision Console**:
   - Public-sector administrative table with statutory review statuses (`Review`, `Monitoring`, `Approved`).
   - "View Evidence" modal displaying verbatim farmer voice records, satellite telemetry, and cryptographic audit trails.
   - AI Insight Panel with human-in-the-loop governance safeguards.

6. **Impact Tracking Loop**:
   - Quantifies before vs. after outcomes (e.g., 39.5% reduction in recurring farmer water distress, +30.8% crop yield recovery).
   - Ensures transparent public ROI.

7. **Digital Public Good (DPG) & Responsible AI**:
   - Open standards, modular decoupled architecture, privacy by design, and strict human review requirements.
   - Prominently labeled demonstration and synthetic datasets.
